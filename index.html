How it works

Three taps to a finished form.

  1. Scan or add

    Scan a paper with your camera, or add a photo, PDF or file. Stash-it! names it and files it.

  2. Tap Tools

    Open the document and tap Tools. The tools that fit it are shown first: compress, passport photo, self-attest, merge…

  3. Share or upload

    Save the result, then upload it to the form or share it. It stays on your phone until you do.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Stash-it! free?

Yes. Stash-it! is free to download, with no sign-up and no ads. Scanning, keeping and finding your documents is part of the app. The tools use a few Stash coins: you get 25 free coins every morning, and the first use of every tool is free.

How do Stash coins work?

Each tool uses a few coins. 25 free coins arrive every morning, and the first time you use any tool it is free. If you need more, you can buy a coin pack. There is no subscription.

Can it make my photo under 50 KB or 20 KB?

Yes. Compress to a size brings a photo or PDF down to the size you need, such as 100 KB, 200 KB or 500 KB, or a size you type. Exact size for forms gives you a photo and signature in the size an online form asks for. Always check the form's own instructions for the limit.

Can I self-attest without a printer?

The Self-attest tool puts your signature and the date on every page of a document on your phone, so you can share the PDF without printing. Some offices still ask for a pen signature on paper, so check what your form needs.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Stash-it! works in English and Hindi. Switch the language any time in the one simple Settings page.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Your documents stay on your phone, and nothing is uploaded unless you share it. There is no account. You can lock the app and your Vault with a PIN or fingerprint, and keep an automatic backup in a folder you choose so you can restore after reinstalling.

Is it easy for my parents to use?

That is what it is made for: big buttons, one home screen, one simple Settings page, bigger text with one tap, and documents that open in a smooth, plain scrolling view. It is light and quick on everyday phones.
